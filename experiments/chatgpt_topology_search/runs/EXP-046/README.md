## Question

Place the two shunt inductors at the *physical* ends and the two main-series inductors next to one another, maximizing their opportunity for adjacent magnetic coupling under |k|<=0.10. The 4 inductors are LQP02HV Q25, 0.4..27nH. Tunable capacitors have Q35, each 0..3pF, three branches in six settings. Existing fixed external input and output S2P, 50-ohm source, 60-j40-ohm load, and all 28 disconnected bands are retained.

## Computation / limited evidence

The in-chat JavaScript analysis used original GitHub-main S2P rows covering all bands, complex real/imaginary interpolation at the source grid, exact nodal Schur reduction and ABCD cascade. Inductor admittance is `B (diag(L/Q)+j M)^(-1) B^T / (omega*1e-9)`, with L in nH and physical-neighbor signed k. Capacitor admittance is `omega*1e-12*C / (1/Q-j)`, C in pF. The baseline historical EXP-042 pcb_cf1 29-element vector was reproduced on 28x31 exactly (IL 0.811306585764dB, H2 30.020000dB, H3 30.020000dB). This is an **internal check**, not new independent-MNA validation.

1. **Same electrical EXP-042 graph, physical row reordered:** old L0-L1-L3-L2 to new L1-L0-L3-L2, physically Sh-S-S-Sh. New coupling sequence k(L1,L0),k(L0,L3),k(L3,L2), with all other nominal pairs zero. Without retuning: IL 0.848973dB; H2 23.196dB / H3 19.790dB. Two local coordinate-search initializations, 868 total coarse 28x5 evaluations, then 28x31 check: best IL 0.876383dB / H2 29.014dB / H3 26.837dB. **Fails strict 30dB.**
2. **Re-synthesized pi-type electrical topology:** L0 IN->center and L1 center->OUT; shunt L2 IN->tip and L3 OUT->tip. Physical row L2-L0-L1-L3. Two GJM01 shunt caps, two PCB pattern caps, three IC tunables. Three local starts, 2,009 coarse evaluations and 28x31 checks. Lowest-penalty sampled point IL 1.588224dB/H2 28.255dB/H3 28.530dB; lowest IL trial 1.078591dB/H2 27.619dB/H3 28.964dB. **Neither passes 30dB.**

Both investigated 4L configurations retain chip area 5⅓. The selected 29-real-variable arrays, branch definitions, and both failed target evaluations are in `exploratory_vectors.json`. Numerical values are exploratory only: they were generated by in-chat JS coordinate-search and **the JS program, CI rerun, full 28x1001/7001 verification, and independent branch-current MNA of new vectors have NOT been archived or performed**. Do not promote these diagnostics to verified candidates or claim a global topology lower bound.

## Interpretation and next topology direction

Chip-L resistive losses are proportional to `sum((omega*Li/Q)*|Ii|²)`. Adjacent positive central-series mutual coupling can increase driven magnetic-energy Q if currents are aligned, but merely increasing k neither reduces ohmic resistance per coil nor guarantees 30dB transmission-zero placement. On the reordered original graph, harmonic constraints worsen severely; the pi-type graph gains a separate shunt resonator at each end but also shows poor rejection in the sampled search. Further RF-T005 phase-2 exploration should modify both capacitive cross-connections and where series-vs-shunt resonances create independent zeros; first recover 30dB before assessing IL and k saturation. Physical realization, SPD/branch-MNA/power-balance for new vectors, tolerance, SRF, and Q(f) remain unverified. Phase 3 remains blocked.

**No baseline RF-F003 or RF-T005 conditions were relaxed.**

### Research conclusion / gating

The strict 30dB pi-4L point **does not improve** EXP-042/RF-T005 area5⅓ `pcb_cf1` baseline worst IL **0.811306586dB**, as the new pi control is **1.323175101dB**. Its margins to nominal35dB and interim0.8dB are also negative. The extra PCB-C scans and mixed-shunt variants all violate H2/H3. Some examples have passband IL under 0.8113dB in isolation but **cannot be counted as compliant improvements**; e.g. third PCB-C shuntL2-parallel IL 0.802286dB has only H2 22.498dB and H3 16.940dB.

The physical theory that positive series-series mutual coupling can raise driven magnetic-energy Q is not contradicted; it cannot by itself guarantee useful stopband zeros or lower full-system GT loss. In the next Phase-2 search the priority is a qualitatively different 2-stop-mode synthesis that retains low-Q branch current suppression while placing H2 and H3 zeros independently. Continuous-band extremes, manufacturer Q(f)/SRF, actual mutual k/dot layout, EM and tolerances remain open. **Phase 3 remains stopped.**
